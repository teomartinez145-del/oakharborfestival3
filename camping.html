Camping

Stay close to the music all weekend. Camping spots are available by donation, positioned within view of the East Stage — every dollar helps keep the main festival free for the community.

Pricing

Choose the option that fits your crew. All spots are first-come, first-served once reserved.

Good to Know

Portable restrooms & water access

No electrical or water hookups

No outdoor burning

Leashed pets welcome

Check-out: Monday at noon

Spaces are positioned within viewing distance of the East Stage, with portable restrooms and access to public water on-site. There are no electrical or water hookups, and outdoor burning is not permitted. Pets are welcome if leashed, per Washington State law.

Camping fees are a donation, not a standard service — they're what let Oak Harbor Music Festival stay free and open to everyone else on the island.
