Music Scholarships

Every dollar raised at Oak Harbor Music Festival goes back into the community — funding music scholarships for students pursuing music.

Who's Eligible

Scholarships are open to students from three island high schools: Oak Harbor High School, Coupeville High School, and South Whidbey High School.

The Scholarships

Three scholarships, each named for someone who gave Whidbey Island students a lifetime of music. Click a card to see every winner since 2013.

How to Apply

Scholarships are awarded each year to students at Oak Harbor, Coupeville, and South Whidbey High Schools. Check with your school's music department for this year's application details, or reach out to us directly with questions.
