More Than Just Music

The Full Festival Experience

Two Live Stages

The Island Thrift East Stage and Peoples Bank West Stage keep the music going from midday into night.

Food Trucks & Beer/Wine Garden

Local food vendors and our Beer and Wine Garden keep everyone fed, hydrated, and happy.

Arts & Crafts Vendors

Browse handmade work from island artists alongside the music all weekend long.

On-Site Camping

Stay close to the action all weekend — spots are available by donation within view of the East Stage. See camping options >

Family & Kids Zone

Activities and a welcoming atmosphere make the festival a favorite for island families.

Accessible Parking & Services

Special accessible parking near the main entrance, with staff on hand to help all weekend.

Good to Know

Oak Harbor Music Festival is free and open to everyone, made possible by our sponsors, donors, and volunteers. Donations are always welcome!

General parking is available near the festival grounds, with special accessible parking close to the main entrance for guests who need it.
